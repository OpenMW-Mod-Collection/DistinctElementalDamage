# Distinct Elemental Damage (OpenMW)

New secondary effects for elemental damage. To spice things up and make the choice of which element to use even more interesting.

We already have weaknesses and resistances to certain effects, but I feel like we can go a step further - by introducing secondary effects to elemental damage.

- **Fire** - Increases received physical damage
- **Frost** - Drains Speed
- **Shock** - Damages Magicka
- **Poison** - Deals extra Health damage

The mod works on both you and your enemies.

## Compatibility

The mod works purely in the background and doesn't edit any records, so it shouldn't conflict with anything.

Safe to install, update, or remove mid-playthrough.

## Requirements

The latest OpenMW 0.52 Dev Build.
