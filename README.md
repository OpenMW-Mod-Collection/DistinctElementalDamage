# Distinct Elemental Damage (OpenMW)

New secondary effects for elemental damage. To spice things up and make the choice of which element to use even more interesting.

We already have weaknesses and resistances to certain effects, but I feel like we can go a step further - by introducing secondary effects to elemental damage. What if Poison was worth its egregious cost, Frost slowed enemies down and Shock damaged their magicka? Well, now you can have it all.

Secondary effects can be assigned to any elemental damage type and include:

- Physical weakness
- Drain Speed
- Damage Health
- Damage Magicka
- Damage Fatigue

Secondary effects work on both you and your enemies and respect elemental resistances.

## Compatibility

The mod works purely in the background and doesn't edit any records, so it shouldn't conflict with anything.

Safe to install, update, or remove mid-playthrough.

## Requirements

The latest OpenMW 0.52 Dev Build.
